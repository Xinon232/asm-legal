Privacy Policy

Another Stupid Monday
Last updated: 8 July 2026

Another Stupid Monday ("ASM", "the app", "we", "us") is a wellness and self-care app that presents reflective exercises and weekly check-ins. This policy explains what information the app handles and is intended to satisfy Apple App Store and Google Play privacy-policy requirements.

Short version

  • ASM is designed to work without an account.
  • ASM does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, journal entries, health records, or payment-card number.
  • Progress and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple App Store, Google Play, and RevenueCat. We do not receive or store your payment-card details.
  • ASM does not currently use advertising SDKs, third-party analytics, or third-party crash-reporting services.
  • ASM is a wellness/self-care app, not medical care, therapy, diagnosis, treatment, or crisis support.

1. Information stored on your device

ASM stores app state locally so the app can remember where you are. This may include onboarding status, current/active exercise and completed exercise identifiers, weekly completion/check-in state and related dates, coins, shop items, cosmetic/unlock state, language, sound, music, font-size and notification settings, and purchase/access state cached by the app so paid access can be recognized.

This information is stored in the app's local device storage. It is not intentionally sent to us by ASM.

If you write reflections in a physical notebook or outside the app, those notes remain yours and are not read by ASM. The app is not designed to collect journal text, therapy notes, or medical records.

2. Purchases, Google Play Billing, App Store, and RevenueCat

ASM may offer paid access or lifetime access through in-app purchases. Payment and purchase processing is handled by the platform where you installed the app: Apple App Store / StoreKit on iOS, Google Play Billing on Android, and RevenueCat for purchase validation and access/entitlement management.

We do not receive or store your full payment-card number, bank details, or other payment credentials. Apple, Google, and RevenueCat may process purchase identifiers, transaction receipts, subscription/access status, country/store region, device/app technical data needed for purchase validation, and related diagnostics according to their own privacy policies.

Relevant third-party policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

3. Notifications and permissions

ASM can schedule local reminders, such as a Monday reminder. Notification permission is controlled by your device settings. Local reminders are generated on your device and do not require us to collect your personal information.

Depending on platform/version, ASM may request or use notification permission, Internet/network access to open policy/support links and communicate with app-store purchase services/RevenueCat, and the Google Play Billing permission on Android to make and restore purchases through Google Play.

ASM does not request access to contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, photos, calendars, or health sensors for its core app experience.

4. Data collection and sharing

ASM itself is designed not to collect personal data on our servers. Because purchases are handled by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat, those providers may collect and process purchase-related and technical data as necessary to complete purchases, prevent fraud, restore access, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data. We do not share personal data for third-party advertising. We do not use the app to send health information, journal content, or therapy notes to advertisers or analytics providers.

5. Google Play Data Safety notes

For Google Play disclosure purposes, the current app behavior is intended to be represented as follows, subject to the final Play Console form wording:

  • No account creation required.
  • No ads.
  • No intentional collection of name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, photos/videos, audio, files, calendar data, location, health data, journal text, or sensitive personal information by ASM.
  • App activity/progress and settings are stored locally on the device.
  • Purchase-related data is processed by Google Play Billing and RevenueCat to provide paid access and restore purchases.
  • Data is not sold and is not shared for advertising.

If a future version adds analytics, crash reporting, accounts, cloud sync, in-app journaling, advertising, or other data collection, this policy and the app-store privacy forms will be updated before release.

6. Support emails

If you contact us for support, we will receive the information you choose to include in your email, such as your email address, message, screenshots, device details, or app-related context. We use this information only to respond to your request and improve the app.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it, reset the app, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. You can also change notification permissions in your device settings.

Purchase records may be retained by Apple, Google, RevenueCat, payment processors, or tax/accounting systems according to their legal, fraud-prevention, and operational requirements. For support emails or other information you sent directly to us, you can request deletion by contacting us.

8. Security

ASM minimizes the personal data it handles by keeping progress and settings local where possible. No method of electronic storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but ASM is designed to avoid collecting sensitive personal content in the first place.

9. Children's privacy

ASM is intended for a general wellness audience and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a related support request, contact us so we can address it.

10. Health, wellness, and crisis disclaimer

ASM provides wellness and self-reflection content only. It is not medical care, psychotherapy, diagnosis, treatment, emergency help, or a substitute for professional advice. If you may harm yourself or someone else, or you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a qualified crisis hotline immediately.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes or legal/store requirements change. The "Last updated" date above will show the latest version.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, support, or data requests, contact us at monday@halim-jarrar.de.
