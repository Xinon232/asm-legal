Safety information

Another Stupid Monday provides general wellness and self-reflection content. It is not a medical device and is not intended to provide medical care, psychotherapy, diagnosis, treatment, emergency assistance, or crisis support. It does not replace advice, diagnosis, or treatment from a qualified healthcare professional.

If you may harm yourself or another person, are in immediate danger, or need urgent help, contact local emergency services or an appropriate crisis service immediately.

EU labels and markings

Another Stupid Monday is supplied as a software-only digital application through the Apple App Store. It is not a physical consumer product and has no packaging, physical safety label, or physical conformity marking.

No EU product-safety label or marking is displayed for the app. In particular, the app is not marketed as a medical device and no CE marking is claimed for it.

Use and support

Please keep your device and operating system updated, use the app only as intended, and stop using it if it does not function as expected. For product-safety or compliance questions, or to report a concern, contact monday@halim-jarrar.de.
